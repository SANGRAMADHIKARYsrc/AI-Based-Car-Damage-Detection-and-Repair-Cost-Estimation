How is this estimate generated?

The estimate uses detected damage characteristics (component type, damage severity, damaged surface footprint) combined with vehicle specifications (OEM tier, body style, drivetrain) evaluated by a Gradient Boosted Regressor. It is intended for preliminary assessment and may differ from an actual workshop quotation.

Dataset Sources & Limitations

Dataset

  • Detection: Annotated vehicle damage images with bounding box labels
  • Cost model: Copart salvage vehicle auction records (historical US market data)
  • Severity: 3-class labeled crop patches (minor / moderate / severe)

Known limitations

  • System evaluates visible external damage only
  • Hidden structural or mechanical damage cannot be detected
  • Single-image input may not capture full extent of damage
  • Repair cost varies by region, parts availability, and labour rates
  • Model performance depends on training data quality and image clarity
  • Output is an estimate, not a professional quotation

Research transparency

This is an academic research prototype. Metrics shown are from development evaluation and may not generalize to all vehicle types. The system is not certified for insurance or legal use.

CarDamage AI

AI-Based Car Damage Detection and Repair Cost Estimation

An applied AI system that performs automated vehicle damage detection, semantic damage classification, component-level spatial reasoning, and preliminary repair-cost estimation using multimodal deep learning.
